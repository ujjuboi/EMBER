EMBER Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What we collect

EMBER is a local-first workout tracker. Everything you create in the app — your account, profile, workout history, plans, partner data, and custom exercises — is stored on your device in an on-device database. There is no EMBER server, no cloud account, and no analytics tracking. Your data is never uploaded.

Health data

With your explicit permission, EMBER reads your daily step count from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) so you can see your progress toward your step goal. EMBER only reads steps and never writes health data. You can revoke this access at any time in your device's Health / Health Connect settings.

Partner sharing

If you choose to pair with a partner, your display name, workout history, and step count are sent directly to that partner's device over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection. No third party ever sees this data.

Advertising

EMBER contains no advertising, no ad trackers, and no third-party analytics.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at the address listed on the app store listing for the version you installed.
